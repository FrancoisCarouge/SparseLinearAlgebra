nodiscardconsteval

For each row of the Cholesky factor of a pattern, the set of its columns, a bit per column.

The up-looking symbolic factorization: a row of the factor stores its diagonal, and the columns reached from each column of the lower triangle of the same row of the pattern, by climbing the elimination tree, built along the way, up to the row or to a column already reached for that row. The climbs of a row never visit a column twice: the computation is linear in the stored positions of the factor.
