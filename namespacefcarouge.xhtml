Detailed Description

Francois Carouge's projects namespace.

The definitions and implementations of the library intended for public usage: the sparse matrix, its operations, and the sparsity pattern concept. Lowers the name conflict probability in large projects. Use using-declarations or namespace-alias-definition per your project guidelines.
